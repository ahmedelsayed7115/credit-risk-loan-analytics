The Lending Problem

Three underwriting questions the credit committee needed solved

With nearly 1 in 4 loans (146 of 601) entering default, underwriting rules relied on broad averages rather than empirically validated FICO, DTI, and tenure cutoffs.

01 · CREDIT & DTI CUTOFFS

Where do FICO and DTI default risks inflect?

Subprime borrowers (520–599) default at 49.1%, while risk drops sharply at FICO 700 (16.3%) and escalates above 40% DTI (27.6% → 36.0% at 60%+ DTI).

02 · PURPOSE & TICKET SIZE

Which loan purposes and ticket sizes amplify loss?

Wedding (32.1%) and Home Improvement (28.6%) lead in frequency, while Medical ($30.4K), Education ($29.2K), and Debt Consolidation ($29.0K) defaults carry 38%–71% larger ticket sizes.

03 · EMPLOYMENT STABILITY

Does job type or tenure predict default better?

Employment tenure (<2 years at 34.5% default vs. 2–4 years at 17.5%) is a nearly 2× stronger discriminator than employment category alone.

Pillar 1 · Credit Score & Debt-to-Income Diagnostics

FICO 700 and DTI 40% define the portfolio's natural risk boundaries

Cross-tabulating borrower credit profiles against loan outcomes reveals a 4.2× default multiplier between subprime (<600) and super-prime (750+) tiers, compounded by a +10.8 percentage-point DTI gap.

While discretionary Wedding loans exhibit the highest default incidence (32.1%), non-discretionary categories default when loan principal stretches 38% to 71% above normal category averages.

Underwriting Recommendations

Five policy rules tied directly to portfolio default metrics

1

Enforce FICO 600 Minimum Floor for Unsecured Loans

Require full collateral or decline applicants in the 520–599 band where 57 of 116 loans defaulted.

49.1% Rate57 defaults intercepted
2

Set 50% Hard DTI Ceiling & 40% Conditional Review

Cap standard approvals at 40% DTI (11.9% default in 20–40% band) and block unsecured originations above 60% DTI.

36.0% → 11.9%10.8-pt DTI gap closed
3

Cap Principal Amounts on Education, Medical & Consolidation

Require secondary review when requested principal exceeds $22,000 in categories where defaulters borrow $29K–$30.4K.

+43% to +71%Ticket-size spike curbed
4

Early-Tenure (<2 Years Employed) Income Verification

Mandate co-signers or 6-month reserve verification for applicants employed under 2 years (34.5% default vs. 17.5% at 2–4 yrs).

2.0× Risk29 early-tenure defaults
5

Risk-Adjusted Pricing for Discretionary Wedding & Auto Loans

Apply +150–250 bps rate premiums and tighter DTI caps (<40%) on Wedding (32.1% default) and Part-Time (27.7%) segments.

32.1% PeakHighest purpose default
